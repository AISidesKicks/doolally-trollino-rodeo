**Doolally**

* **Role:** Moderator
* **Tagline:** *"Did somebody say BAN?!"*
* **Appearance:** A stressed, grumpy cave troll wearing a high-visibility safety vest with a "MOD" badge, a headset mic, and mismatched green-and-orange scanner goggles.
* **Vibe & Abilities:** Constantly on the edge of a burnout, Doolally patrols the digital dungeon looking for trouble. Armed with glowing "BAN" stamps, "FILTER" shields, and "BLOCK" buttons, he ruthlessly deletes spam and enforces the number one rule: *No Feeding!*
* **Special Move:** **The Banhammer** - Instantly vaporizes rule-breakers and locks down the comment section.

**Trollino**

* **Role:** Author (The Provocateur)
* **Tagline:** *"Just asking questions... 😉"*
* **Appearance:** A mischievous, grinning troll sporting wild rainbow hair, purple eyebrows, a bone earring, and a tattered vest.
* **Vibe & Abilities:** Chaos incarnate. Trollino lives to stir the pot, typing away on his stone laptop with a literal fishing hook labeled "BAIT" attached to his desk. He hurls pixelated fireballs and red exclamation marks into the chat just to watch the comment section burn.
* **Special Move:** **Master Baiter** - Drops a take so outrageous that even the calmest users can't resist replying.
